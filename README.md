# CTF Track Record — Official Competitions Only

This repository records **real CTF competitions that were actually attended**. It is the event-level evidence log, not a practice journal and not the home for full challenge write-ups.

## What belongs here

- One concise record per competition in [`competitions/`](competitions/README.md).
- Event name, organizer, date/timezone, individual/team format, public team/participant name, official scoreboard/result URL, and verified score/rank when available.
- A link to a research artifact in [`reverse-engineering-lab`](https://github.com/madanisora/reverse-engineering-lab) (link to a specific experiment only after it exists) if an observation from the event later motivates a separate experiment.

## What does not belong here

- Self-paced platform practice, crackmes, and independent lab exercises: keep their **full write-ups** in [`ctf-writeups/practice/`](https://github.com/madanisora/ctf-writeups/tree/main/practice).
- Duplicate copies of challenge solutions or research reports.
- Planned registrations presented as participation, or scores/ranks/certificates without verifiable evidence.

## Evidence rules

Never invent participation, dates, teams, scores, ranks, certificates, solved challenges, or research results. Follow event publication rules and do not publish prohibited flags, solutions, private information, or third-party artifacts without permission.
